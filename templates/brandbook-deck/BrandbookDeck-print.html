01 — Positioning
We build, evolve, and scale AI-powered digital platforms.
A long-term technology partner for companies investing in digital products, data, and AI — not a vendor for a single release.
02 — Brand Archetype
The Magician
BluePixel turns complex, unproven ideas into working digital products — the transformation from "concept" to "concept, then design" to shipped platform. Confidence comes from making change feel inevitable, not risky.
Secondary: The Sage
Expertise-led, data-informed, allergic to hype. Claims are specific and earned — "within 24 hours," not "revolutionary."
03 — Voice & Tone
Direct, not jargon-heavy
"We shipped it in three weeks." Not "We leveraged an agile methodology to deliver synergistic outcomes."
Confident, not hyped
Back claims with a number or timeframe. Skip "revolutionary," "game-changing," "cutting-edge."
Human, never corporate
"We" for BluePixel, "you / your platform" for the client. Contractions are fine. Social copy can be playful and punny in Spanish.
Product copy stays clear
UI microcopy is short and verb-first. In-app empty/error states are helpful, not cute — playfulness lives in the Pixelios, not in error messages.

05 — Logo
Built on a pixel structure — the cyan square anchors the wordmark. Keep clear space equal to the square's height on every side; never stretch, recolor, or drop shadow the mark.

08 — Iconography
A 13-glyph UI set — mixed outline and filled shapes, always rendered in currentColor. No emoji, no unicode icons; brand personality lives in the Pixelios, not the UI glyphs.
09 — Pixelios
Our brand characters
The Pixelios are BluePixel's cast — a pixel-square character family, not mascots. They carry warmth into decks, social and onboarding without touching UI copy.
